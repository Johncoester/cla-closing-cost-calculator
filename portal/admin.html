Pricing tables: CLA settlement fee only

A pricing table is a named set of lower Settlement Fee to CLA Title & Escrow amounts, for example "Marvin's Pricing". Add a rep's email to a table and that rep's quotes, and the quotes of the clients (realtors) assigned to that rep, use the table's fees. Title insurance premiums, recording fees, taxes and every other fee never change. Anyone not in a table pays the standard fees.
