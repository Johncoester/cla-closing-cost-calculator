1Welcome

This guide shows you how to get a closing cost estimate, save it, and print or share it.

PartWhat it is
Closing cost calculatorGives you an estimate of the closing costs for a purchase, a cash purchase or a refinance, and what a seller would walk away with. Anyone can use it, signed in or not.
Your quotes pageWhen you sign in, the quotes you run are saved to your account. This page lists them so you can open, print or delete them. It also shows your profile and your CLA rep.
An estimate is not a guarantee. It is not a Loan Estimate, a Closing Disclosure or a promise to insure. For a binding quote, ask your CLA rep.

2Signing in

There is no public sign-up. CLA sends you an email invitation, and you sign in with that same email address.

Your first time: the invitation email

  1. Open the email from CLA and click the linkThe link works for 24 hours. If it has lapsed, see "Link expired or forgot your password" below.
  2. Choose a passwordAt least 12 characters, with upper and lower case letters and a number. Type it twice.
  3. Accept the privacy notice and termsTick the box, then choose Create my account.
  4. Finish your profileAdd your name and company. A photo is optional. See "Your profile and photo".

Signing in after that

  1. Open the sign-in pageGo to quotes.clatitle.com/portal/login.html, or choose Log in at the top of the calculator.
  2. Enter your email and passwordUse the email address CLA invited. Choose Sign in.
  3. You land on your quotes pageFrom there choose New quote, or Calculator in the top bar, to run another.

Link expired or forgot your password

  1. Open the sign-in page and choose Forgot password?
  2. Enter your email and choose Send reset linkYou always see the same message, whether or not the email is on file: "If that email has a CLA portal account, a reset link is on its way." You can ask for another link after about a minute.
  3. Open the newest email and choose a new passwordReset links also last 24 hours. A used or expired link says "This link has expired or was already used. Request a new one from the sign-in page." Choose Forgot password? again for a fresh one.

To sign out, choose Sign out in the top bar.

3Getting an estimate, step by step

Open the calculator from the top bar (Calculator) or with New quote on your quotes page. You can use it without signing in, but only signed-in quotes are saved.

  1. Pick Buyer Quote or Seller Net SheetBuyer Quote shows what a buyer pays at closing. Seller Net Sheet shows what a seller keeps. Both are explained below.
  2. Pick the type of transactionPurchase is a financed sale. Cash has no loan. Refinance replaces the sale price with the payoff of the old loan and the amount of the new one.
  3. Enter the ZIP codeThe state and county fill in by themselves, because county taxes depend on them. The address and city are optional and show on your quote.
  4. Check the county or cityYou can change it in the County / city list.
  5. Enter the price and the loan amountOn a refinance, enter the payoff and the new loan amount.
  6. Choose the policy optionsOwner's policy, who pays for it, and how fees are shown.
  7. Tick endorsements and check the documentsBoth are under Fees & options. The starting choices match a typical purchase, so change only what applies to you.
  8. Read the estimate on the rightIt updates with every change you make. Then save, print or copy it (see "Saving, printing and sharing a quote").

The inputs in plain words

InputWhat it does
Purchase / Cash / RefinancePurchase: a financed sale. Cash: no loan, so no lender's policy and no lender-only fees. Refinance: a payoff and a new loan, with no owner's policy and no seller column.
Property address, CityOptional. They print on your quote and appear on the saved quote.
ZIPFive digits. It fills in the state and county. If a ZIP covers more than one county, the page says so. If a ZIP is not on file, you pick the state and county yourself (CLA covers MD, VA, DC and FL).
State, County / cityMD, DC, VA or FL. The county or city decides county recording fees and transfer taxes. Until you choose one, the page says "Enter a ZIP or choose a county to include county taxes."
UnderwriterThe title insurance company whose policy is quoted.
Sale price, Payoff, Loan amountThe price is not used on a refinance. Payoff and loan show on a refinance. Loan is hidden on a cash purchase.
Owner's policyEnhanced / Homeowner's or Standard coverage.
Reissue or basic optionReissue means a title policy on the property already exists, which can change the premium. Leave it on Basic if you are not sure.
Owner's policy paid byLocal custom (what is usual in that county), Buyer or Seller.
Fee presentationLoan Estimate / CD (TRID) shows premiums the way the lender's forms do. Actual premium split shows what each person really pays.
First-time homebuyerApplies a first-time buyer exemption or treatment where the county has one. Not used on a refinance.
Owner-occupied principal residenceOn by default. Some exemptions depend on it. In Maryland, a long deed that is not a principal residence can record differently, and a note on the page says so.
Buyer pays seller's transfer / deed taxA contract term, for example Florida deed stamps. It moves the seller's tax to the buyer column.
Documents to recordHow many Deeds, Deeds of Trust, Releases and Powers of Attorney, and how many pages each. The starting numbers match a typical CLA quote.
EndorsementsOptional extra coverage. A few are ticked to begin with. The choices depend on the state, the underwriter and the type of transaction.
CLA feesLocked: you cannot edit them. They are tagged Buyer or Seller and come from CLA's current fee list.

Seller Net Sheet

Choose Seller Net Sheet to see what a seller keeps. Address, ZIP, state, county and price carry across when you switch.

  • Property and sale: address, ZIP, state, county, selling price and closing date.
  • Commissions: the buyer's broker and the seller's broker, each as a percent or a dollar amount.
  • Payoffs and property taxes: first mortgage payoff, second mortgage or HELOC, yearly property taxes and the date they are paid through. Taxes are shared by days: the seller owns every day up to the day before closing, using a 365-day year.
  • Credits and other costs: seller concession, HOA dues owed, HOA transfer fee, HOA special assessment, home warranty, other costs, and up to five lines of your own, such as repairs.
  • Title and settlement: who pays the owner's policy, whether the buyer is a first-time homebuyer, and whether the buyer pays the seller's transfer tax.
  • Result: net proceeds are the selling price minus the seller's total costs minus the mortgage payoffs.

4What your results mean

The estimate on the right of the calculator, and the same layout on a saved quote.

How the page is laid out

  • The big number at the top is the buyer total (the borrower total on a refinance). Under it are the seller total and the all-parties total.
  • Below that, lines are grouped under letters: B Services you cannot shop for, C Services you can shop for, E Taxes and other government fees, H Other. Each group has a buyer column (the borrower, on a refinance), a seller column and a group total.
  • An Estimate total closes the page. The line under the headline names the transaction, county, state and underwriter.
  • If a line has a dash in a column, nothing is charged to that person for it.
  • If the line under the headline says BUILT-IN SNAPSHOT, the page could not reach CLA's latest fee list. Choose Reload team sheet to try again.

What each kind of line is

LineWhat it is
Title insurance: Lender's policyInsurance that protects the lender's loan. Only when there is a loan.
Title insurance: Owner's policy (optional)Insurance that protects the buyer's ownership of the property. Not on a refinance. Marked "simultaneous-issue" when it is bought together with a lender's policy.
EndorsementsExtra coverage added to a title policy.
Settlement fee (closing fee) to CLA Title & EscrowCLA's fee for handling the closing.
Other CLA feesSmaller fees for the work around a closing, such as preparing documents, notary, secure document storage, title review, title search and abstract, a closing protection letter (lender only), and electronic recording.
Recording feesWhat the county charges to record each document (a deed, a deed of trust, a release) in the land records. Shown as "x2" and so on when there is more than one.
Transfer and recordation taxesState, county or city taxes paid when ownership or a loan is recorded. Who pays follows local custom and your contract; some are split.
Warnings boxA yellow box that appears when something could not be calculated, for example no recording fee found for a county. Treat the total as incomplete until you understand the warning, and ask your CLA rep.

An estimate, not a guarantee

  • Included: the title insurance, CLA, recording and tax lines shown on the page, for the choices you made. Anything not listed on the page is not in the total.
  • Not included: charges from other companies, such as a lender's own fees, and anything the calculator does not list. Your lender's Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure are the documents to rely on for those.
  • Why the numbers can change by closing: you may change a choice (price, loan amount, who pays for the policy, the documents to record), the county or city may be different, CLA's fee list may be updated, or the final contract terms may differ.
  • The footer on every printed and saved quote says it is an estimate, not a Loan Estimate or Closing Disclosure, and that actual charges may vary. For a binding quote, contact CLA.

5Saving, printing and sharing a quote

Here is everything the system offers for keeping a quote and passing it on.

Saving

  • While you are signed in, your quote saves by itself about 2.5 seconds after you change something. Opening the page does not save anything. It keeps updating the same quote.
  • The note under the buttons says "Saved to your account", shows the date the quote will delete itself, and links to View with photos and My quotes.
  • Save as new quote makes a second copy, so you can keep this version and keep changing the first. The Buyer and Seller tabs save as separate quotes.
  • If you are not signed in, the button says Log in to save. After you sign in, a confirmation page saves the quote you were working on.
  • Your CLA rep can see the quotes you save, so they can help you with questions. The confirmation page says so.

Print or save as a PDF

  1. Choose Print / Save PDFOn the calculator, or at the top of a saved quote. Your browser's print window opens. The printed page leaves out the input boxes and buttons and shows a clean copy of the estimate, with the CLA header.
  2. To keep a file, pick Save as PDF as the printerTo print on paper, pick your printer.

Copy the quote as text

On the calculator, choose Copy quote. It puts a plain-text version on your clipboard, with the address, the type of transaction, the county, every line, the totals and the estimate-only note. Paste it into an email or a text message. If your browser blocks copying, a box opens with the text so you can copy it yourself.

What is not offered

There is no button to email a quote from the portal, no share link and no separate download button. The ways to get a quote out are Print / Save PDF and Copy quote. If you would like CLA to send a quote to someone, ask your CLA rep.

6Opening and using your saved quotes

Choose Dashboard in the top bar any time to come back to your quotes page.

  • My profile: your name, company and contact details, with a link to edit them and your photo.
  • My CLA rep: your rep's name, photo and phone numbers (see "Your CLA rep").
  • Saved quotes: each quote with its type (Buyer quote, Refinance or Seller net), price, the date saved, and how long it has left.

Open, print or delete a quote

  1. Choose View / print on the quoteThe quote opens with your photo and your rep's photo at the top, the headline total, every section, and any warnings.
  2. Choose Print / Save PDF to keep a copySee "Print or save as a PDF" above. Choose Back to return to your list.
  3. To remove a quote, choose DeleteYou are asked to confirm. A deleted quote cannot be brought back.

What you see on a quote

On the quoteWhat it means
Label (for example "Buyer quote, 123 Main St")A name the system gives the quote from its type and the address you entered, so you can tell quotes apart in your list. With no address it says "no address".
Address and "Prepared" dateThe property you entered and the day the quote was first saved.
"Fees:" lineWhich version of CLA's fee list the estimate used, with its date.
Headline totalThe buyer total (borrower total on a refinance), or the net proceeds on a seller net sheet.
"Auto-deletes ... (N days left)"The day the system will remove the quote, 30 days after it was created. Editing a quote does not restart that clock. It is highlighted when only a few days are left.

A quote does not show a "valid until" date. The auto-delete date is only about how long the system keeps your copy, not how long the numbers can be relied on. Ask your CLA rep how long an estimate holds.

Using a saved quote when you talk to others

  • With a buyer or seller: send the PDF or the pasted text, and point to the headline total first, then to the section letters (B, C, E, H) for the details.
  • With a lender: give them the PDF so they can compare the title and settlement lines with their own Loan Estimate. Differences are normal until the details are final.
  • With an agent: the label and address make it easy to match a quote to a property. Say which transaction type you chose.
  • Always say it is an estimate. The date prepared and the "Fees:" line show how current it is.
  • Print or copy anything you want to keep: quotes delete themselves after 30 days. If you open one that is gone, you see "That quote was not found."

7Starting a new quote and comparing

Run as many quotes as you like. Each signed-in quote is saved separately.

  1. Choose New quote on your quotes page, or Calculator in the top barA fresh calculator opens.
  2. Compare two situations with Save as new quoteSet up the first situation, choose Save as new quote, then change the price, loan amount, county or other choices. The first stays saved as it was, and the changes go into a new copy.
  3. Check your listEach quote shows its type, price and the date saved, so you can open them one after another and compare the totals.
  4. Changing the numbers laterA saved quote is opened to view and print, not to edit. To change it, run it again in the calculator. While you stay on the calculator page, changes keep updating the same saved quote.

Tip: print or copy the version you want to keep before you start changing things.

8Your profile and photo

Open your name in the top bar, or choose Edit profile and photo on your quotes page.

  • First and last name are required. Add your company so quotes carry your details.
  • Phone, company phone, company address, website and license / NMLS number are optional and print on your quotes.
  • Your cell phone is optional. It must be a US number, for example (240) 555-0142, and your CLA rep can see it.
  • To change the email you sign in with, contact CLA.

A photo is optional. Use a JPEG or PNG up to 2 MB. The portal crops it to a centered square and shrinks it to 512 pixels. iPhone HEIC photos are not accepted yet: take a screenshot of the photo or save it as a JPEG first. Choose Choose photo, then Upload this photo. Choose Remove photo to go back to the placeholder.

9Your CLA rep

The rep CLA has matched you with is your first call for any question about a quote.

  • Your quotes page has a My CLA rep box with your rep's name, photo, cell phone (tap it on a phone to call), office phone and email address (click it to write an email).
  • Your rep's name and number also print on your quotes.
  • If the box says you have not been matched to a CLA rep yet, your quotes still save, and CLA will connect you by email.
