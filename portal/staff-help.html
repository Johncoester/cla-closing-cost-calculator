1What the system is

For everyone

Two tools that work together: a public closing cost calculator and a private quote portal.

PartWhat it isWho can use it
CalculatorThe closing cost estimator at the web address quotes.clatitle.com. It builds a Buyer Quote (purchase, cash or refinance) or a Seller Net Sheet from CLA's fee tables.Anyone, signed in or not
Quote portalThe sign-in area at quotes.clatitle.com/portal/login.html. It saves your quotes, connects realtors and lenders ("clients") to their CLA rep, and gives CLA staff the tools to manage people, pricing and feedback.Invited people only. There is no public sign-up.

What a quote is

A quote is a saved copy of what the calculator showed at that moment: the inputs you chose, every fee line, the totals, any warnings, and which fee table was used. A quote is either a Buyer quote (including cash and refinance) or a Seller net sheet. Signed-in people have their quotes saved automatically, and each quote deletes itself 30 days after it was created.

What a quote is not

Every number is an estimate. It is not a Loan Estimate, not a Closing Disclosure and not a commitment to insure. Actual charges may vary. For a binding quote, contact C.L.A. Title & Escrow.

Who sees what

PersonSees
Guest (not signed in)The calculator only. Guest quotes are not saved.
Client (realtor or lender)Their own saved quotes and their CLA rep's name, photo and phone numbers.
RepTheir own quotes and the saved quotes of the clients assigned to them.
Admin or super adminAll users, all saved quotes, all assignments, the audit log, staff feedback and the pricing tables.

Photos follow the same idea: only you, CLA admins, and your assigned CLA rep or client can see your photo.

2Getting started

For everyone

Sign in, accept an invitation, reset a password, take the tour and send feedback.

Sign in

  1. Open the sign-in pageGo to quotes.clatitle.com/portal/login.html, or choose Log in in the calculator header.
  2. Enter your email and passwordUse the email address CLA invited. Choose Sign in.
  3. Land on your dashboardAdmins and super admins open the Admin dashboard, reps open the Rep dashboard, clients open their quotes page.

Your invitation email

CLA invites you by email. The email comes from the CLA Quotes mailbox. The link in it opens a Welcome to CLA page.

  1. Click the link in the invitation emailThe link expires in 24 hours.
  2. Choose a passwordAt least 12 characters, with upper and lower case letters and a number. Type it twice.
  3. Accept the privacy notice and termsTick the box, then choose Create my account. The notice text is a placeholder until CLA's counsel approves the final wording.
  4. Finish your profileAdd your name and a headshot. Reps must also enter a phone number.

Link expired, or forgot your password

  1. Open the sign-in page and choose Forgot password?
  2. Enter your email and choose Send reset linkYou always see the same message, whether or not the email has an account: "If that email has a CLA portal account, a reset link is on its way." You can ask for another link after about a minute.
  3. Open the newest email and choose a new passwordReset links also expire in 24 hours. A used or expired link shows "This link has expired or was already used. Request a new one from the sign-in page." Forgot password? sends a fresh link.

Your profile and photo

Open your name in the top bar. First and last name are required. Phone is required for reps. Your cell phone is optional and must be a US number; it is shown to your assigned rep or clients and to CLA admins. Clients also add company details that print on their quotes. To change your sign-in email, ask a CLA admin.

A photo is optional. Use a JPEG or PNG up to 2 MB; the portal crops it to a centered square and shrinks it to 512 pixels. iPhone HEIC photos are refused for now. Until you add one, a plain placeholder picture is used.

This staff manual is one click away: choose Help in the top bar of any portal page, or next to your name in the calculator header. Clients get their own shorter client guide from the same Help link; staff can read it too (see the link at the top of this page).

The guided tour

A short click-through in John's voice. In the portal it starts by itself the first time you open your dashboard after finishing your profile, with 6 to 7 steps for your role. Replay it any time with Take the tour in the top bar. The calculator has its own tour (about 13 to 15 steps) for anyone, signed in or not: use Take the tour in the calculator header or the "New here?" chip. Skip with Skip tour or Esc; Back and Next or the arrow keys move.

The feedback bubble (staff only)

Reps, admins and super admins see a round chat bubble in the bottom-right corner. Clients and guests never see it.

  1. Click the bubbleA panel opens with a greeting from John.
  2. Pick a kindBug, Idea, Question or Other.
  3. Write your messageUp to 2,000 characters. The page you are on is added automatically (the page name only, nothing else).
  4. Choose Send feedbackYou see "Thanks, I read every note." You can send up to 10 an hour and 40 a day.

The same panel lists your recent feedback with a status (Received, John has been told, Approved or Closed) and John's note, if he left one.

4Using the calculator step by step

For everyone

Everything on the calculator page, from the first input to printing the result.

Step by step: a buyer quote

  1. Pick Buyer QuoteSeller Net Sheet is covered further down.
  2. Pick the transaction typePurchase is a financed sale. Cash has no loan. Refinance replaces the sale price with a payoff and a new loan amount.
  3. Enter the ZIPThe state and county fill in by themselves, because county taxes depend on them. Address and city are optional and show on the quote.
  4. Check the county or cityYou can change it in the County / city list.
  5. Enter price and loan amountOn a refinance, enter the payoff of the existing loan and the new loan amount.
  6. Choose the policy optionsOwner's policy, rate, who pays, and how fees are shown.
  7. Tick endorsements and check the documentsUnder Fees & options.
  8. Read the estimate on the rightIt updates with every change. Print, copy or let it save.

The inputs

InputWhat it does
Purchase / Cash / RefinancePurchase: financed sale. Cash: no loan, so no lender's policy and no lender-only fees (such as the CPL). Refinance: payoff and new loan, no owner's policy and no seller column.
Property address, CityOptional. They appear on the printed or copied quote and on the saved quote. A ZIP typed at the end of the address line is picked up.
ZIPFive digits. Fills state and county. If a ZIP spans more than one county it says so, for example "This ZIP also covers ...". If a ZIP is not on file you are asked to pick the state and county yourself (CLA covers MD, VA, DC and FL). A ZIP that is not in the list may use the nearest one and asks you to confirm the county.
State, County / cityMD, DC, VA or FL. County or city decides county recording fees and transfer taxes. Until one is chosen the page says "Enter a ZIP or choose a county to include county taxes."
UnderwriterThe title insurance company whose rate table is used. The list comes from the fee sheet.
Sale price / Payoff / Loan amountPrice is not used on a refinance. Payoff and loan are shown on a refinance; loan is hidden on a cash purchase.
Owner's policyEnhanced / Homeowner's or Standard coverage.
RateBasic, or Reissue (a prior policy exists, which can lower the premium).
Owner's policy paid byLocal custom (the usual practice in that county from the fee sheet, buyer if none), Buyer or Seller.
Fee presentationLoan Estimate / CD (TRID) shows premiums the way the lender's forms do. Actual premium split shows what each party really pays.
First-time homebuyerApplies a first-time buyer rate, exemption or payer where the county rules have one. Not used on a refinance.
Owner-occupied principal residenceOn by default. Some exemptions depend on it. In Maryland, a deed or deed of trust of 10 or more pages that is not a principal residence records for $115 instead of $60, and a note says so.
Buyer pays seller's transfer / deed taxA contract term (for example Florida deed stamps). Moves the seller's tax to the buyer column.
Documents to recordHow many Deeds, Deeds of Trust, Releases and Powers of Attorney, and how many pages each. The defaults match CLA's usual quote: purchase one deed (3 pages) and one deed of trust (30 pages); cash one deed; refinance one deed of trust and one release.
EndorsementsOptional extra coverage. Some are ticked by default. Choices depend on state, underwriter and type.

What each fee line means

Lines are grouped under letters: B Services you cannot shop for, C Services you can shop for, E Taxes and other government fees, H Other. Each section shows a buyer column (borrower on a refinance), a seller column and a section total.

LineWhat it isNotes
Title - Lender's Title PolicyInsurance protecting the lender, based on the loan amount.Only when there is a loan. Calculated from the underwriter's rate bands, with a minimum premium. With TRID presentation it shows the full lender premium; with Actual split it shows the reduced simultaneous-issue amount.
Title - Owner's Title Policy (optional)Insurance protecting the buyer, based on the price.Not on a refinance. Enhanced, Standard, Basic or Reissue change the premium. Marked [TRID simultaneous-issue] when it is bought with a loan policy.
Title - Endorsement ...Extra coverage added to a policy (for example ALTA forms).A flat amount or a percentage of the premium. Florida Form 9 is 10% of the owner's plus loan premium.
Title - Settlement Fee to CLA Title & EscrowCLA's closing (settlement) fee.Different for buyer, seller and refinance. This is the only line that special pricing can change.
Other CLA feesDoc preparation, notary, secure document storage, title review and data collection, title search and abstract, CPL (closing protection letter, lender only), processing, shipping and tax certificate fees on refinances, and e-recording.Amounts and which party pays come from the CLA_Fees tab. E-recording is charged once per recorded document.
Recording fees - Deed, Deed of Trust, Release, Power of AttorneyThe county clerk's charge to record each document.A flat fee plus an amount for each page beyond those included, times the number of documents. Shown as "x2" and so on.
Transfer and recordation taxesState, county or city taxes on the sale or the loan.Rates, brackets, exemptions, first-time buyer treatment and who pays all come from the Transfer_Taxes tab. Buyer and seller columns follow who pays; some are split.
Warnings boxA yellow box that appears when something could not be priced.For example no premium rates for a state, or no recording fee or transfer tax found for a county. Treat the total as incomplete until you understand the warning.

How county and city differences work

  • County-specific rows in the fee sheet override the statewide rows with the same name.
  • If a county has no rows of its own, the page says "Using state default rates for this county".
  • DC is a single jurisdiction. Virginia lists counties and independent cities.
  • The default for who pays the owner's policy comes from each county's entry in the Jurisdictions tab.

Reading the results

The big number is the buyer total (the borrower total on a refinance), with the seller total and the all-parties total underneath. Below it, each section lists its lines, and an Estimate total table closes the page. The line under the total names the transaction, county, state and underwriter, and shows BUILT-IN SNAPSHOT if the live sheet could not be loaded.

Seller Net Sheet

Switch to Seller Net Sheet to see what a seller keeps. Address, ZIP, state, county and price carry across when you switch tabs.

  • Property and sale: address, ZIP, state, county, selling price, closing date.
  • Commissions: buyer's broker and seller's broker, each as a percent or a flat dollar amount.
  • Payoffs and property taxes: first mortgage payoff, second mortgage or HELOC, annual property taxes and the date taxes are paid through. Taxes are prorated: the seller owns every day through the day before closing, using a 365-day year. If you leave the date blank, a typical schedule for the state is assumed and the page tells you which.
  • Credits and other costs: seller concession (dollars or percent), HOA dues owed, HOA transfer or resale fee, HOA special assessment, home warranty, termination or other fees, and up to five lines of your own such as repairs.
  • Title and settlement: whether the seller pays the owner's policy (starts from local custom), whether the buyer is a first-time homebuyer, whether the buyer pays the seller's transfer tax, and the CLA seller fees.
  • Result: net seller proceeds equal the selling price minus total seller costs minus mortgage payoffs. Cards list commissions, government tax and recording fees, title and settlement fees, payoffs, prorations and credits, and other costs.

Saving, copying and printing

  • Signed in: the quote saves by itself about 2.5 seconds after you change something (never just from opening the page). It keeps updating the same quote. Save as new quote starts a second copy. Buyer and Seller tabs save as separate quotes.
  • The note under the buttons reads "Saved to your account", shows the date it will delete itself (30 days after it was created), and links to View with photos and My quotes.
  • Guest: the button says Log in to save. After you sign in, a confirmation page saves the quote you were working on.
  • Copy quote puts a plain-text version on your clipboard to paste into an email. Print / Save PDF opens your browser's print dialog and prints a clean copy.

Photos on quotes

Signed-in clients and reps see a co-branded bar under the mode switch: the client's photo, name and contact details on one side (company, phone, cell phone, company phone, company address, website and license / NMLS number, only the ones the client filled in on their profile), and their CLA rep's photo, name and cell phone (or office phone) on the other, with "Prepared with C.L.A. Title & Escrow". It also prints. A plain placeholder picture shows until someone adds a photo. A plain admin has no photo bar; an admin who also works as a rep gets one like a rep.

Special rate

If your pricing includes a lower settlement fee, the CLA fees panel shows the lower amount with "(special rate, standard $...)" beside it. Only CLA's settlement fee changes. Printed, copied and saved quotes show the amount without the word special. See section 8.

5Quotes in the portal

For everyone

Saving, finding and who can see which quotes.

  • Saving: automatic while signed in (section 4). A quote deletes itself 30 days after it was created. The clock never restarts when you edit it.
  • Finding yours (clients): your quotes page lists each saved quote with its type, price, saved date and how many days are left. View / print opens it; Delete removes it for good after a confirmation.
  • Finding clients' quotes (reps): on My clients, click a client to see their saved quotes, or open the Quotes tab for all clients' quotes plus your own.
  • Finding any quote (admins): the Quotes tab lists everyone's quotes, with a search box for client, rep or address. A quote a client deleted shows a Deleted by client badge. Opening someone else's quote is written to the audit log.
  • Opening a quote: a page with the co-branded header, the headline total, every section, warnings and the date it will expire. It has a Print / Save PDF button.

Reassigning

Quotes are not moved one by one. A rep sees the quotes of the clients assigned to them, so when an admin moves a client to a different rep, the new rep sees that client's quotes straight away and the old rep no longer does. Each quote also remembers which rep the client had when it was created, which admins see in the Rep column.

Admin / super adminAll live quotes
EveryoneQuotes older than 30 days are hidden immediately and removed by a nightly clean-up. Admins lose them at 30 days too.
A rep can still see a client's quotes if that client is disabled, until the client is reassigned.

Invite a client

  1. Open My clients and find Invite a client or agent

  3. Choose Send inviteThe page says the invitation was recorded. The client gets an email to set a password and is matched to you, so the quotes they save show up on your dashboard.

The Quotes tab lists your clients' quotes and your own. An admin who also works as a rep sees only their own clients on the Rep dashboard.

7Admin guide

For admins

People, roles, assignments, the audit log and staff feedback. Open it from Admin dashboard.

Tabs: Users, Quotes, Assignments, Audit log, Feedback and Rep pricing. Everything you do here is written to the audit log.

Invite a user

  1. Choose Invite userThe invite form opens.
  2. Enter the email and choose a roleSales rep (CLA email required) or Real estate agent / client. Only a super admin also sees Admin and Super admin.
  3. Optional extrasA cell phone, Also works as a rep for a new admin or super admin (CLA email), and Assign to rep for a client.
  4. Choose Send inviteThe person gets an email from the CLA Quotes mailbox. The link works for 24 hours; if it lapses they use Forgot password? on the sign-in page for a fresh one.

Invite a client for a specific rep

Admins and super admins can invite a realtor or lender and attach them to a rep in one step, so the rep does not have to send the invite.

  1. Open Admin, then Users, and choose Invite user
  2. Enter the client's email and set Role to Real estate agent / clientThe Assign to rep (optional) list appears under the role.
  3. Pick the sales rep in the Assign to rep listLeave it on No rep yet if you want to assign them later on the Assignments tab.
  4. Choose Send inviteThe client gets the set-password email. They are assigned to the rep you picked, so that rep sees them and their quotes on the Rep dashboard.
  • If the rep you picked has a pricing table, the client sees that table's settlement fee when they sign in (unless the client's own email is in a table, which wins).
  • Who can invite whom: admins can invite reps and clients only. Only a super admin can invite admins and super admins; an admin does not see those options and the server refuses the request if one is sent.

Roles, enabling and disabling

  • Change a role with the dropdown on the person's row and confirm. An admin sees Rep and Client for reps and clients, and Admin, Rep and Client for another admin. Only a super admin can promote someone to admin or super admin.
  • Disable stops a person signing in. Their data stays. Re-enable brings them back. You cannot disable or demote yourself, an admin cannot touch a super admin, and the last active super admin can never be disabled or demoted.
  • Super admin rows show a fixed badge and a greyed-out button, with a tooltip that explains why.
  • Also works as a rep is a checkbox on admin and super admin rows. Only a super admin can change it.

Demoting someone who has clients

A person who works as a rep and still has active clients cannot stop being a rep (made a client, or Also works as a rep switched off) until their clients move. The page opens "Move N realtors to..." with a list of other eligible reps and how many clients each has. Pick one and continue; all clients move at once and the history is kept. Cancel or Esc changes nothing. If no other rep is available, invite or enable one first.

Assignments

Enter the client's email, pick a rep, and choose Save assignment. It matches by email, so it works before the client has an account. Reassigning keeps history: the table shows each assignment as Active or Previous.

Quotes

The Quotes tab lists every saved quote with a search box for client, rep or address (see section 5).

Audit log and last sign-in

The Audit log is read-only and newest first, with When, Who, Action and Details. It records invitations, role and status changes, the rep switch, assignments, photo changes, quote deletions and admin views of quotes, the nightly purge, feedback and pricing table changes. Nobody can edit or delete entries.

In the Users table, the Status badge tells you whether someone has signed in: Active (hover to see the last sign-in date), Invited, never signed in (they have not opened the invitation yet), or Disabled.

Staff feedback review

Admins and super admins read every item and can filter by status and category or search. Only a super admin can add a note and choose Approve or Dismiss; admins see it read-only. The sender sees the status and note in their feedback panel. Statuses are New, Notified, Approved and Dismissed.

8Special pricing: pricing tables

For admins

Lower CLA settlement fees for a rep or an individual client. Settlement fee only.

The rules

  • A pricing table has a name, for example "Marvin's Pricing".
  • Only the line Settlement Fee to CLA Title & Escrow can change. Title insurance, endorsements, recording fees, taxes and every other CLA fee never change.
  • A table holds fee sets. A fee set is a group of states (any mix of MD, VA, DC and FL, or All states) with up to three fees: purchase buyer pays, purchase seller pays and refinance borrower pays.
  • A blank box means the standard fee. A fee of $0 waives that line, and the line disappears.
  • A state can be in only one fee set of a table. The All states set is a fallback for any state with no set of its own, and cannot be ticked together with single states.
  • For a quote in a state, the set that contains the state wins as a whole. If it leaves a line blank, that line is standard, not the All states fee.
  • Add members to a table by email: a rep, or an individual client. One email can be in only one table. The person does not need an account yet.
  • Whose fee applies: first the person's own email table, then the assigned rep's table, otherwise standard fees. So a client in their own table beats their rep's table.
  • Guests always see standard fees. An admin or super admin gets special pricing only if they also work as a rep.
  • Only admins and super admins can create or edit tables; every change is in the audit log.

Create and fill a table

  1. Open Admin, then Rep pricing
  2. Choose New pricing table, type a name, then Create table
  3. Open the table and choose Add fee setTick states or All states. The standard fee appears beside each box. Fill the boxes you want to change, then Save fee set.
  4. If a state is already in another setThe page says so and offers Move to this set.
  5. Add membersType a rep's or client's email and choose Add. Each row shows whether they have an account, their role, and for a client whether they use this table or follow their rep. An email already in another table offers Move to this table.
  6. Test itSign in as the rep, or one of their clients, and quote a ZIP in a covered state.

Create "Marvin's Pricing" with three fee sets, then add the rep marvin.rep@example.com and the client agent.one@example.com.

  • On the printed, copied or saved quote only the amount appears, with no word special.
  • The Seller Net Sheet uses the seller's special settlement fee too.
  • If anything goes wrong loading pricing, the calculator quietly uses standard fees.

9Where the fee data comes from

For admins

A Google Sheet that the calculator reads every time the page loads.

Every number comes from the sheet named CLA Fee Tables (Live - Calculator). Rows only count when their Active column is Y. A change to the sheet shows for everyone the next time they load the calculator or choose Reload team sheet.

TabWhat it holds
CLA_FeesCLA's own fees by state, transaction and who pays, including the settlement fee.
Recording_FeesCounty recording fees per document, with page tiers and a source link.
Transfer_TaxesTransfer and recordation taxes by state and county: rates, brackets, exemptions, first-time buyer rules, who pays.
Premium_TiersTitle insurance rate bands by underwriter, state and policy type.
Premium_RulesSettings such as minimum premiums, rounding and simultaneous-issue rules.
EndorsementsEndorsement codes, names and prices.
JurisdictionsCounties and cities, with who customarily pays the owner's policy.
  • If the sheet cannot be loaded, the calculator falls back to a built-in snapshot, shows a yellow warning and marks results BUILT-IN SNAPSHOT. Rates in a snapshot can be out of date.
  • Recording fee watch is a daily check of the county source pages listed in the sheet. It only reports changes for a person to review; it never edits the sheet or the calculator by itself.
  • Title insurance premiums follow each underwriter's rate manual (for example the Maryland rate manual) and, in Florida, the state's promulgated rates. The source is noted on the sheet rows.
  • The built-in snapshot is refreshed from the sheet by hand with CLA's developer tools.
